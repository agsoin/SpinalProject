CHARGE-AF

Cohort for Heart and Aging Research in Genomic Epidemiology — AF Risk Score

The CHARGE-AF score is the most widely replicated prediction model for newly diagnosed/incident AF per ACC/AHA guidelines. It uses individual clinical variables as model features.

Note on age range: CHARGE-AF was calibrated on cohorts aged roughly 45–85 (CHS, ARIC, Framingham). The age input below is pre-filled from your date of birth entered earlier. The model's published performance is strongest within this range — it remains informative outside it but with somewhat reduced calibration.

C₂HEST Score

AF Prediction Score — ACC/AHA Guideline Recognised

The C₂HEST score (0–8) is an ACC/AHA AF guideline recognised prediction instrument. Each positive criterion adds points toward your total score.

C₂ = CAD/COPD (2 pts) · H = Hypertension · E = Elderly ≥75 · S = Systolic HF · T = Hyperthyroidism

AFEQT

Atrial Fibrillation Effect on QualiTy-of-Life — 20-Item Validated Questionnaire

The AFEQT is a 20-item validated AF-specific quality-of-life questionnaire with strong internal consistency. It covers AF symptoms, daily activity limitations, treatment concerns, and treatment satisfaction.

For each item, rate how much AF has bothered you during the last 4 weeks. 1 = Not at all bothered · 7 = Extremely bothered
Domain 1 — AF Symptoms
Domain 2 — Daily Activities
Domain 3 — Treatment Concerns
Domain 4 — Treatment Satisfaction

CCS-SAF Scale

Canadian Cardiovascular Society — Severity in Atrial Fibrillation

The CCS-SAF is a simple clinician-rated 0–4 ordinal scale validated in 484 patients with documented AF. It correlates with AF symptom severity, quality of life and healthcare utilisation. Please select the description that best matches your experience.

AFSymp

Validated 11-Item Patient-Reported AF Symptom Questionnaire — 1-Week Recall

AFSymp is a validated 11-item questionnaire with a one-week recall period. It covers heart symptoms, tiredness, chest discomfort, dizziness, and shortness of breath. Please rate how much each symptom bothered you in the past 7 days.

0 = Not at all · 1 = A little · 2 = Quite a bit · 3 = A great deal
